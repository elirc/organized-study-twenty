# Two-Week Cram Plan

Assumes ~2.5h/day weekdays, ~4h weekend days. Everything references materials in this suite. "Aloud" means actually aloud — recorded if possible; the gap between silent and spoken fluency is the interview.

## Week 1 — Build the anchored knowledge

**Day 1:** [00-fast-track.md](../00-fast-track.md) reading path (skip install if time-boxed) + [Flow 1](../01-codebase-cartography/05-key-flows.md) traced with files open. Aloud: 3-minute create-record walkthrough.
**Day 2:** Flows 3 & 4 (auth, webhooks). Cards [01-js-ts-node](01-js-ts-node-deep-dive.md) Q1–Q5 aloud.
**Day 3:** [03-architecture/03 (permissions)](../03-architecture-and-patterns/03-validation-auth-and-permissions.md) + [03/04 (reliability)](../03-architecture-and-patterns/04-side-effects-async-and-reliability.md). Cards Q6–Q10.
**Day 4:** [03/06 critique](../03-architecture-and-patterns/06-architecture-critique.md) — rehearse the 4-minute "system I know well" delivery twice. Cards Q11–Q14.
**Day 5:** Frontend day: [02-stack/02](../02-stack-and-language-mastery/02-framework-mental-models.md) + all [02-frontend cards](02-frontend-framework-questions.md) aloud.
**Day 6 (weekend):** Full mock **system design** ([04-system-design](04-system-design-from-this-repo.md)) on a whiteboard/paper, 45 min timed, then variations 3 and 5. Then 4 annotation drills from [04-code-reading-gym/01](../04-code-reading-gym/01-annotation-drills.md).
**Day 7 — checkpoint:** Self-assess against [09-reference/learning-rubrics.md](../09-reference/learning-rubrics.md). Can you: draw the layer stack cold? explain webhook delivery semantics with anchors? give the four-beat answer (definition→example→tradeoff→failure) for 10 random cards? Weak areas set Week 2's emphasis.

## Week 2 — Convert knowledge to performance

**Day 8:** Timed debugging round [D2](05-debugging-and-code-review-rounds.md) aloud + API cards [03-api](03-api-and-data-modeling-questions.md) Q1–Q6.
**Day 9:** Timed review rounds R1 + R3 aloud. API cards Q7–Q11.
**Day 10:** STAR day: fill all 8 [worksheets](06-behavioral-star-stories.md) with your real material; rehearse stories 1, 2, 5 to under 2 minutes each.
**Day 11:** Timed debugging D1 + D3. Re-run the 5 weakest cards from Day 7's checkpoint.
**Day 12:** Second full system-design mock (variation 1 or 4 as the main prompt this time). Rehearse the critique delivery once more.
**Day 13 (weekend):** Full loop simulation: 30 min screen (random 6 cards) → 45 min design → 25 min debugging (D4, unseen till now) → 20 min behavioral (stories 3, 7). Debrief in writing: what ran long, what lacked anchors.
**Day 14 — final checkpoint:** Redo the Day 7 checks + one teach-back: explain Flow 6 (custom fields) to a non-engineer in 5 minutes. Pack list for interview day: your 3 strongest stories, the layer-stack diagram, the webhook tradeoff, the tenancy table.

## Rules of the fortnight

- Every answer aloud gets the four-beat structure; no exceptions.
- When you don't know, practice the honest bridge: "I haven't used X in production; here's how I'd reason about it from Y" — rehearsed honesty outperforms improvised bluffing.
- Skip nothing on Days 7 and 14; the checkpoints *are* the plan.
- Stop at your time budget daily. Cramming past fatigue burns Day-13 performance, which is the day that predicts the real loop.
